Optical Trapping / Thermal modeling

Using UCNPs to Determine Optical Trap Temperature Gradients for 980 nm Gaussian Beams

Working with upconversion nanoparticles for optical trapping in order to make temperature gradient estimations. Lab Advisor: Bjorg Larson balarson@wm.edu

In this project we plan to use the emission spectra of UCNPs to measure the temperature gradients of our optical trap.

as well as confirm our simulated lasers accuracy.

Biomedical Optics / Ocular Straylight Quantification

Using theoretically predicted decay patterns of posterior subcapsular cataracts to estimate straylight metrics quantitatively with hydrogel comparison.

A project focused on modeling and comparing scatter behavior to quantify straylight in a structured way. Lab Advisor: Bjorg Larson balarson@wm.edu

This project focuses on quantifying a measured quantity in opthamology, straylight.
