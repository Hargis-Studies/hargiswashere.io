Research

Optical Trapping Current Research

Using UCNPs to Determine Optical Trap Temperature Gradients for 980 nm Gaussian Beams

This project uses upconversion nanoparticles (UCNPs) to investigate temperature gradients produced by optical trapping with 980 nm Gaussian beams. Emission spectra from the nanoparticles are used as a potential experimental measurement of the temperature distribution within the trap.

Research advisor: Bjorg Larson · balarson@wm.edu

The experimental measurements will also provide a means of evaluating the accuracy of our simulated optical-trap temperature distributions.

Biomedical Optics Current Research

Quantifying Ocular Straylight from Posterior Subcapsular Cataract Scattering

This project investigates the scattering behavior associated with posterior subcapsular cataracts and explores whether theoretically predicted decay patterns can be used to estimate straylight metrics quantitatively.

Research advisor: Bjorg Larson · balarson@wm.edu

The broader goal is to connect measurable scattering behavior with a quantity used in ophthalmology: ocular straylight.

Scattering Physics Jan 2025 – Jun 2025

Modeling Light Propagation Through Whole Milk Using Monte Carlo Methods and Mie Theory

A study of light scattering and propagation through whole milk using Mie theory, Monte Carlo modeling, and experimental measurements.
